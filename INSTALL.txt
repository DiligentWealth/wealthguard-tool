What changed:
1. On screens at least 1100px wide, section navigation is fixed on the left, with
   reserved space so it cannot cover the calculator. On smaller screens, it stays
   at the top as a horizontally scrollable sticky strip.
2. Saved Scenarios opens immediately above Calculation Mode.
3. Calculation Mode is a compact strip. Show planning basis / Hide planning basis
   toggles the detailed explanations. Existing validation warnings stay visible.
4. Portfolio Projection includes Explore planning changes. It compares the plan
   captured when opened with a temporary what-if plan. Controls cover:
   - Retirement age for each client, with +/- one-year buttons and age inputs.
   - Total annual spending including NZ Super.
   - Reduced later-retirement income, percentage and years after first retirement.
   - Additional care costs and annual amount, using the recorded start/duration.
   - The recorded first-retirement-year market shock.
   - Today's-dollar or future-dollar chart display.
   The reference and what-if lines use the SAME end date and display final
   balances, unfunded spending/withdrawals and whether all targets are met.

Preview workflow:
Load the scenario, go to Charts, and click Explore planning changes. Adjust the
controls. Reset preview discards temporary edits. Close preview returns to the
existing graph. Apply preview to calculator updates the main inputs explicitly;
it adjusts the retirement horizon to retain the common end date. Then Save
Current under Scenarios to retain it and include it in normal report comparisons.
Preview changes are not automatically saved or exported in a report.
Re-run Monte Carlo after applying changes.

Retirement-related events move relative to the new first-retirement date.
Accumulation payments stay in their recorded years. If an age change would move
one-off payments outside the modelled period, the preview shows an error and
blocks applying it rather than silently discarding the payment.
